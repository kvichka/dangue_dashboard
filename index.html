L2Cumulative Cases, 2026 vs. Selected Year

Counts only, never rates — a cumulative rate over a partial year is not comparable with a full one. The running total skips unreported months rather than carrying a flat line through them, so a flat segment means no cases were reported, not that no report arrived.

N1Outbreak Signal — 2026 Monthly Cases Against the 2018–2025 Baseline

Counts, not rates — the threshold is defined on counts. Baseline is 8 prior years (2018-2025), built only from months that actually reported, so each month rests on between 7 and 8 years.
Two different "2025" numbers appear on this page — they are not the same thing. Charts labeled "YTD" compare 2026 to the same reporting week in 2025 (weekly DHF bulletin). The charts above and the Multi-Year table (Map & Table tab) instead use full calendar-year 2018–2025 totals from the historical line-list.

N9Province × Month Heatmap — Cases

Which provinces peak early and which peak late — directly relevant to Wolbachia release timing. Click a province name to set the filter. Incidence rate for the year is in the last column.

August to December 2026 are blank because they have not been reported yet — not because there were no cases.
Data quality note: the source CFR2025 column in the DHF weekly file was computed against the wrong year's case denominator (mixing 2025 deaths with 2026 cases). CFR2025 figures here are recalculated correctly as Deaths2025 ÷ Cases2025.
